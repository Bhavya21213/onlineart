Project Title: Online Art Gallery

Project Description:
The Online Art Gallery is a full-stack web application designed to provide a digital platform for artists to showcase their artwork and for art enthusiasts to explore, appreciate, and purchase
various forms of visual art. This platform bridges the gap between creators and collectors by offering a seamless, interactive, and user-friendly interface for viewing and managing art collections online.
Built with modern web technologies, the project emphasizes responsiveness, accessibility, and performance to ensure an engaging experience across devices.

Key Features:
 Artwork Showcase: Artists can upload and manage their artworks, including images, descriptions, and categories.
 Artist Profiles: Dedicated pages to highlight artist biographies, portfolios, and social media links.
 Advanced Search & Filters: Users can search by category, medium, price, or artist for easier navigation.
 Favorites & Wishlist: Users can mark artworks they like and maintain a wishlist.
 E-commerce Functionality: Secure artwork purchases through integrated payment gateways.
 Contact & Commission Requests: Form-based inquiries for buying, feedback, or custom artwork commissions.
 Admin Dashboard: Role-based access for managing users, artworks, and site content.

Objectives:
To support independent and professional artists by offering a centralized platform to manage and promote their artwork.
To enable art lovers to browse a wide range of artistic styles and mediums through an intuitive and visually rich interface.
To provide seamless interactions between buyers and sellers, enhancing accessibility in the art market.

Project Outcomes:
Delivered a clean, scalable, and professional online gallery experience.
Improved accessibility to the art market for both creators and buyers.
Gained practical experience in full-stack development, user authentication, file handling, and real-world deployment workflows.


Tech Stack:
Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, Bootstrap
Backend: Django
Version Control: Git & GitHub
Purpose & Impact:
This project aims to empower independent artists by giving them a free and scalable platform to display their work, build a fanbase, and even earn through digital exposure. 
It also provides users a curated experience of appreciating global art without physical constraints.
